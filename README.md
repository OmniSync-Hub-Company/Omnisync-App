# Omnisync-App
OmniSync seamlessly connects your store to Klaviyo, offering advanced RFM segmentation, native cohort dashboards, automated back-in-stock triggers, and intelligent WhatsApp routing—all from one unified workspace.
​Unlock the Full Potential of Your Customer Data.
Stop leaving money on the table with generic marketing. OmniSync Marketing Hub is a premium middleware application designed specifically for Salla merchants who want to scale their retention and conversion strategies. By bridging the gap between Salla’s powerful e-commerce engine and Klaviyo’s advanced marketing automation, OmniSync turns raw order history into actionable, high-converting campaigns.
​Whether you are looking to recover abandoned carts via WhatsApp, reward your VIP customers, or visualize your exact marketing ROI without leaving your Salla dashboard, OmniSync provides the enterprise-grade tools you need to grow your brand.

​🚀 Key Features & Benefits
​1. Intelligent WhatsApp Routing
Reach your Saudi and GCC customers where they are most active. OmniSync intelligently routes high-priority alerts—such as high-value abandoned carts or exclusive VIP rewards—directly through the WhatsApp Business API. Bypass crowded email inboxes and engage shoppers instantly for higher open rates and faster conversions.
​2. Automated RFM Customer Segmentation
Stop guessing who your best customers are. The moment you install the app, our robust data engine analyzes your historical Salla orders to automatically group your customers into actionable tiers (e.g., Champions, At-Risk, Hibernating, Needs Attention). These dynamic segments are pushed directly to Klaviyo, allowing you to trigger hyper-targeted campaigns instantly.
​3. Native, Interactive Analytics Dashboard
Say goodbye to spreadsheet fatigue. OmniSync features a lightning-fast, highly responsive analytics dashboard embedded right within your Salla workspace. Track customer cohort retention over time, monitor Average Order Value (AOV) by segment, and view the direct revenue generated by your automated flows in a clean, intuitive interface.
​4. High-Intent Automation Triggers (Back-in-Stock & Price Drops)
Capitalize on buyer intent the second your inventory changes. OmniSync allows you to capture "Notify Me" requests on out-of-stock items. The instant you update your Salla inventory, the app automatically triggers a Klaviyo flow to notify those specific customers, securing sales before they bounce to a competitor.
​5. Seamless, Real-Time Data Synchronization
Your data, always up to date. OmniSync handles the heavy lifting by syncing historical data during onboarding and utilizing asynchronous webhooks to instantly update Klaviyo with real-time Salla events (Customer Created, Order Placed, Checkout Started).
